FIXMYBACK MANUAL
================

What this is
------------
A Mac background timer. If you sit at the computer for 30 minutes
without a real break, it covers the screen and makes you stand and
stretch for 1 minute.

"Real break" = no mouse/keyboard for 3+ minutes (you walked away).


RUN (start it, keep it on after reboot)
---------------------------------------
    cd ~/Mostafa/fixmyback
    ./install.sh

That loads a LaunchAgent. It starts now and every time you log in.

Quick test (overlay in ~20 seconds, then Ctrl+C):

    python3 ~/Mostafa/fixmyback/sit_timer.py --test

Faster test:

    python3 ~/Mostafa/fixmyback/sit_timer.py --sit-seconds 10 --stretch-seconds 3


STOP
----
Stop forever (won't auto-start on login):

    ~/Mostafa/fixmyback/uninstall.sh

Stop only the test you just ran in Terminal:

    Ctrl+C

See if it is running:

    launchctl print gui/$(id -u)/com.fixmyback.sittimer | head -n 20

If you see "state = running", it is on.


READ THE LOG
------------
Do not type the path alone. That tries to "run" the file.

    cat ~/Library/Logs/fixmyback.log
    tail -f ~/Library/Logs/fixmyback.log


HOW IT BEHAVES
--------------
- Using mouse/keyboard = sitting. Clock counts.
- Idle 3+ minutes = you stood up. Clock resets. Snoozes refill.
- Mac slept / lid closed = you were away. Clock resets on wake.
- Screen locked = it waits. No lock screen until you are actually back.
- After 30 minutes: full-screen lock. Wait 1 minute, then click
  "I stretched".
- Busy? Click "Busy — remind me in 5 min" or press Space.
  You get 2 snoozes. After that you must stretch the full minute.

Honest limit: Cmd+Tab can still leave the overlay. Don't force-quit
if you actually want the stretch.


CHANGE THE TIMERS
-----------------
Edit ~/Mostafa/fixmyback/sit_timer.py near the top:

    SIT_LIMIT_SECONDS   = 30 * 60     sit before lock
    STRETCH_SECONDS     = 60          how long you must stretch
    BREAK_IDLE_SECONDS  = 3 * 60      idle that counts as a break
    SNOOZE_SECONDS      = 5 * 60      snooze length
    MAX_SNOOZES         = 2           snoozes per cycle

Then restart the agent:

    launchctl kickstart -k "gui/$(id -u)/com.fixmyback.sittimer"
